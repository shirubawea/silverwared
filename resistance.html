RESISTANCE
is an EP that I released on December 25, 2023 (Yes, Christmas Day). The cover is a random picture that I had in my camera roll. It's just me posing with the moon but I HIGHLY edited the photo. I turned myself and my surroundings into complete silhouettes, I turned the sky violet and, I turned the moon into a cross kinda. I was kinda into evangelion at the time, I hadn't watched it yet but, I thought it looked really cool. I actually had to remake the cover like 3 times because my computer kept crashing while I was in the middle of editing. My computer really ticked me off during this period of time, there were so many aspects of this project that I had to redo completely because my computer kept crashing all of the time but, I can get to that later.

This is definitely one of my more experimental projects, especially at the time I released it. Before I made RESISTANCE, I used to rap on a bunch of trap beats and stuff. I wasn't very good but by the end of 2023, I became a fan of Yabujin and I started making music that sounded more... I guess mysterious. I don't really know how to explain it.

I was actually very reluctant to put information about this EP on my website because, the lyrics are really bad. I used to rap about self harm alot during this time. Well, I had always done that but after I released my song '1879' and it got pretty popular on Spotify, I just continued with the self harm lyrics. I have since then stopped for obvious reasons but let's start with the songs on this project!

There are 6 songs on RESISTANCE and, 1 of them has a FEATURE!! :D

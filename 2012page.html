2012
is an EP that I released on May 23, 2025. This is the cover for it. This cover is very special because it's the first picture made related to my music that wasnt made by me. My friend Jackson made it for his Graphic Design class, idk what grade he got, or if the assignment even got graded, but I think it's very cool. It shows me holding an iPod Nano 3rd Generation (like on the cover for 2010) but this time I have rusty metal wings on my back. Also the cover was inspired by the VideoNow Color disc cases, but you probably can't really tell on the official cover. If you follow my spam account on ig you've probably seen it already, but to the right is the demo cover that I made back in like December of 2024 (back when I only had 3 songs complete for 2012 lol).

As you can see, the wings look more like they're made out of multiple scraps of metal rather than, idk iron that came out of a mold. Also I'm wearing the funny little justin bieber hat, and I gave myself straight hair.

"Sample": Avatar High

I actually came up with the lyrics for neo first, BUT I recorded geo 2 days before I recorded neo. HOWEVER, I decided to name geo what it is because, I already had the title neo (it's short for Neopets if you couldn't already tell), then I remembered the Neo Geo console (I only know what it is because I've seen an emulator for it on my Wii). If it wasn't obvious already, a lot of the titles of my songs are based off of some other property or whatever.

The beat for neo has a "sample" from a flash game called "Avatar High" which was made by THUP for the-n.com (former website for a nickelodeon sister channel), and by "sample" I mean it's literally just the instrumental from the start of the game, I didn't change anything tbh (I'm a lazy boy (like the couches)). This is probably my LAZIEST beat ever, and I'm sorry to whoever made the tune from Avatar High.

Also, geo is one of my favorite songs by me. ever.

I came up with the title for this song after watching episode (idk) of Big Time Rush, and Griffin (who is the guy that owns the company that owns the record label that owns BTR in the show) told BTR to make a summer song with the word "beach" in it. So it made me wonder, "What if I made a song with the word beach in it?" At first I was just stuck on "summer beach", but I wanted to add something extra to it, and I think I unconsiously came up with the word nightmare from the song "Long Beach Nightmare" by PUFFY (Japanese pop duo, not the evil man).
